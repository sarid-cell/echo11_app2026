2. Information we collect

Account information

If you create an account, we receive an identifier and email address from your chosen sign-in method (Sign in with Apple, Google, or email magic-link). With Sign in with Apple you may use Apple’s private relay email; we never receive your password.

Usage & preferences

To power your streak, history, and saved favorites we store which frequencies you play, your saved favorites, and session length (how long a frequency played). We do not record, store, or transmit any audio, microphone input, or your surroundings — audio is generated mathematically on your device.

Device & diagnostic data

Like most apps, our infrastructure may process basic technical data (app version, device type, coarse region, and error logs) to keep the service reliable and secure.

Guest use

You can use the core app as a guest without an account. In guest mode, preferences such as favorites are stored only on your device.

3. How we use information

  • To provide core features: playback, favorites, streaks, and listening history.
  • To authenticate you and sync preferences across your devices.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and fix bugs.
  • To process subscriptions (handled by Apple — see “Payments”).

4. Tracking & advertising

We do not use third-party advertising and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Because we do not engage in cross-app tracking, the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt does not apply. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by US state privacy laws.

5. How information is processed and shared

We share information only with service providers who process it on our behalf, under contract:

  • Supabase — authentication and database (account, favorites, sessions).
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, and all subscription payments via the App Store.
  • Google — sign-in, if you choose Google.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the service.

6. Payments

Subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple’s In-App Purchase system. We never receive or store your full payment-card details. Apple’s handling of your purchase is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

7. Data retention

We keep account and usage data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the app (About → Log Out, then request deletion), or by emailing us; we will delete associated personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing (GDPR), or to opt out of “sale”/“sharing” (US state laws — note we do neither). To exercise any right, email shirasarid@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

9. Children

echo.|| is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security & international transfers

We use industry-standard safeguards (encryption in transit, access controls). No method is 100% secure. Your data may be processed in countries other than yours; where required, we rely on appropriate transfer safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, in-app notice.
