1. No medical claims

Any descriptions of brainwave states, “binaural beats,” or frequency “protocols” describe a general listening experience and commonly reported associations. They are not clinical claims. Individual responses vary, and no specific outcome is promised or guaranteed.

2. Consult a professional first

Do not use echo.|| as a replacement for care from a licensed professional. Consult your physician before use if you:

  • have epilepsy or any seizure disorder;
  • have a pacemaker or other implanted medical device;
  • are pregnant;
  • have a heart condition, or a psychological or neurological condition;
  • are under the care of a medical professional or taking medication that may be affected by relaxation or sound stimulation.

3. Safe-use guidelines

  • Never use while driving, operating machinery, or doing anything that requires your full attention. Sessions are intended for use while seated or lying down in a safe environment.
  • Keep volume at a comfortable, moderate level to protect your hearing.
  • Stop immediately and discontinue use if you experience any discomfort, dizziness, headache, anxiety, nausea, or unusual sensations — and consult a professional if symptoms persist.
  • echo.|| is not intended for children without adult supervision and professional guidance.

4. Headphones

Binaural frequencies rely on each ear receiving a slightly different tone and therefore require stereo headphones to be experienced as intended. This is an audio-design requirement, not a therapeutic instruction.
