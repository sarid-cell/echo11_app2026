Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Beta status

echo·11 is currently a free, invite-only beta. No payments are collected and no paid subscription is offered.

echo.|| (“echo,” “we,” “us”) makes a sound-based wellness app for relaxation and focus. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. You can listen with no account. If you stay a guest, your listening history stays on this device. If you choose to sign in, a private copy is kept with that account.

1. Who we are

echo·11 is a personal project by Shira Sarid, an independent creator based in Israel — not a registered company. For any privacy question, contact shirasarid@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Account information

You do not need an account to listen. Sign-in sits in Profile, and it never stands in front of playback. If you sign in with Google, or with a link we email you, we store the email address that Google or that link uses. We also store the listening history, saved frequencies, resume point, and breathing streak you choose to keep with the account. There is no password. There is no mailing list, and we do not send marketing email. The link only signs you in.

That account data is stored with Supabase (Supabase Inc.) and is visible only to your account. A logged-out visitor cannot read it, and neither can another account. If sign-in is not set up on the server, the app stays in guest mode and the sign-in option is hidden.

Usage & preferences

To power your streak, history, and saved favorites, the app stores which frequencies you play, your saved favorites, and session length (how long a frequency played) in your browser’s storage, on your own device. If you are signed in, a private copy of that history is also kept with your account so it can follow you to another device. We do not record, store, or transmit any audio, microphone input, or your surroundings. Audio is generated mathematically on your device.

Usage analytics

To learn which frequencies people use, echo·11 uses Google Analytics (Google’s gtag.js) — the same service used on our website, echo11.app. It records anonymous, aggregate events: that a page was opened, that a frequency session was started or completed (its name, frequency and length), plus general information such as device type, browser, referring site and approximate country. Your listening history, favorites and streaks are never sent.

Analytics cookies are off until you choose “Accept” in the app. If you decline, Google Analytics runs without cookies and without identifying your device across visits. You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser. See Google’s Privacy Policy for how Google processes this data.

We do not use tracking pixels or advertising tools.

Hosting & fonts

Like any website, echo·11 is delivered by technical service providers: the site is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA), and its typefaces load from the Google Fonts and jsDelivr content-delivery networks. When your browser requests a page or a font file, these providers receive your IP address and standard request headers, as required to deliver the file, and may keep short-lived technical logs. If you sign in, your account and private listening history are stored by Supabase Inc., and the browser loads the Supabase library from jsDelivr at that moment. A guest does not contact Supabase.

Guest use

You can use echo as a guest. Guest listening stays on this device until you clear it or sign out. Signing in is a choice you make later, from Profile.

3. How we use information

  • To provide core features: playback, favorites, streaks, and listening history.
  • To keep the app reliable and secure.

4. Tracking & advertising

We do not use third-party advertising and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by US state privacy laws.

5. How information is processed and shared

Guest listening history stays on your device. If you sign in, your email and private listening history are stored by Supabase so the same account can see them on another device. We do not sell that data, and we do not use it for a mailing list. Google Analytics still receives only the anonymous usage events described above, not your saved history. If you choose Continue with Google, Google handles that sign-in under its own policy.

6. Payments

None. The beta is free: no payments are collected, no subscription is offered, and no payment information is ever requested.

7. Data retention

Guest data lives in your browser until you remove it. Profile, then Delete my account, clears listening history, streaks, saved frequencies, and preferences from this device. Signing out also clears that history from this device. If you are signed in, the copy in your account stays, and it comes back the next time you sign in.

To delete the account itself, open Profile while you are signed in and choose Delete my account and history. That removes the rows saved to your account and asks the server to delete the sign-in. You can also email shirasarid@gmail.com and ask us to delete it. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes only the copy on that device.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing (GDPR), or to opt out of “sale”/“sharing” (US state laws — note we do neither). To exercise any right, email shirasarid@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

9. Children

echo.|| is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security & international transfers

Guest data stays on your device. Account data is stored by Supabase with row level security, so only that account can read its own rows. The key that can delete a sign-in stays on the server and is never placed in the app. Hosting, fonts, and (only if you sign in) Supabase may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, in-app notice.

Contact
