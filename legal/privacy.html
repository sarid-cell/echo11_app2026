Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Beta status

echo·11 is currently a free, invite-only beta. No payments are collected and no paid subscription is offered.

echo.|| (“echo,” “we,” “us”) makes a sound-based wellness app for relaxation and focus. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We collect as little as possible — during the beta, everything stays on your own device.

1. Who we are

echo·11 is a personal project by Shira Sarid, an independent creator based in Israel — not a registered company. For any privacy question, contact shirasarid@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Account information

During the beta, sign-in only personalizes the app on your own device. No account is created on any server, and no identifier, email address, or password is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Usage & preferences

To power your streak, history, and saved favorites, the app stores which frequencies you play, your saved favorites, and session length (how long a frequency played) — locally in your browser’s storage, on your own device. This data is never transmitted to a server. We do not record, store, or transmit any audio, microphone input, or your surroundings — audio is generated mathematically on your device.

Device & diagnostic data

The beta runs entirely in your browser. We operate no analytics and no tracking pixels, and we do not collect diagnostic data ourselves.

Hosting & fonts

Like any website, echo·11 is delivered by technical service providers: the site is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA), and its typefaces load from the Google Fonts and jsDelivr content-delivery networks. When your browser requests a page or a font file, these providers receive your IP address and standard request headers, as required to deliver the file, and may keep short-lived technical logs. We receive no information from them and none of your in-app data (history, favorites, streaks) is ever sent to them.

Guest use

Guest mode and signed-in mode currently behave the same way: everything is stored only on your device.

3. How we use information

  • To provide core features: playback, favorites, streaks, and listening history.
  • To keep the app reliable and secure.

4. Tracking & advertising

We do not use third-party advertising and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by US state privacy laws.

5. How information is processed and shared

Today: with no one, apart from the technical delivery described above (hosting and fonts), which involves only standard connection data — never your listening data. Because your in-app data never leaves your device, there is nothing for us to share. If a future version syncs data to a server (for example, to back up your history across devices), this policy will be updated first and the change announced in the app.

6. Payments

None. The beta is free: no payments are collected, no subscription is offered, and no payment information is ever requested.

7. Data retention

Your data lives in your browser’s local storage and stays there until you remove it. Delete it at any time from the app — Profile → Delete my account — which permanently clears your listening history, streaks, saved frequencies, and preferences from the device. Clearing your browser’s site data does the same.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing (GDPR), or to opt out of “sale”/“sharing” (US state laws — note we do neither). To exercise any right, email shirasarid@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

9. Children

echo.|| is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security & international transfers

Your in-app data is stored only on your device and is protected by your device’s own security — we transfer none of it. The technical requests described in section 2 (hosting and font delivery) are processed on servers that may be located outside your country, including in the United States.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, in-app notice.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email shirasarid@gmail.com.
