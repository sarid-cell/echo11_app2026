Common questions

The audio doesn't start

Browsers only start audio after a tap. Open a frequency and tap play; if you still hear nothing, check your device isn't in silent mode and the volume is up.

Binaural protocols sound like a plain tone

Binaural frequencies need stereo headphones — each ear receives a slightly different tone, and the "beat" only appears when both channels are separated. Speakers or a single earbud won't produce it.

Where is my data stored?

During the beta, everything — history, streaks, favorites — lives only in your browser's storage on your own device. Nothing is uploaded. Details in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Profile → Delete my account. This permanently clears your listening history, streaks, saved frequencies, and preferences from the device. Clearing your browser's site data does the same.

Is echo a medical product?

No. echo.|| is a wellness and entertainment tool — it does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Please read the safe-use & medical disclaimer, especially if you have epilepsy or a seizure disorder, a pacemaker, or are pregnant.

Safety first

Never listen while driving or operating machinery. Keep the volume moderate, and stop immediately if you feel discomfort, dizziness, or unease. In a medical or mental-health emergency, call your local emergency number — echo.|| does not provide emergency services.
