The Science

The third frequency

Play a tone in one ear and a slightly different tone in the other. Your brain can't separate them — so it generates a third, phantom beat equal to the difference — and the idea is that your own rhythm may begin to drift toward it.

Auditory entrainment

Tuning the brain like an instrument

The idea is called entrainment: the suggestion that the brain can fall into step with a steady external rhythm. echo pairs each protocol with a band and a mood: delta for sleep, theta for meditation, alpha for calm clarity, beta for focus, and gamma for bright, wide-awake attention.

Twenty minutes of simply listening is a chance to slow down — and, with practice, many listeners report easier focus and calmer attention.

The Monroe Institute

Monroe & the Gateway work

Robert Monroe's research foundation pioneered the use of binaural audio to explore focused, coherent states of attention, and built its Gateway training programs on that work — research that inspired echo's approach.
